I finally have the time to sit down and pen down my reflections for the 26th International Conference on Agile Software Development (XP2025).

## A little context of the conference

The International Conference on Agile Software Development, also known as XP, is an event that centers around Agile software development. It is one of the few conference where both academia and industry professionals gather to share their knowledge, expertise and learn from one another.

In 2024, my university hosted the 25th inception (XP2024) and I had the opportunity to be a volunteer. It was my first time volunteering and attending such a major event and I was thrilled. I had only learnt about Agile Software Development not more than a year prior and was excited to witness all the research and practice revolving around the topic. The conference certainly did not dissapoint. I met and conversed with experts in this field and was inspired by their mindset, vision and work. These few days had definitely left a huge impact on me. It was this XP conference that got me interested in exploring deeper into Agile, and choosing it as my thesis topic. Fast forward a year, I found myself once again attending the XP conference, this time also as a speaker. For this experience, I am incredibly grateful for so many people in my life - my supervisor, my co-author, my parents and my partner - who made this possible.

## How I got to present

Many little things got me this chance to present my work in XP2025. For starters, we had a course in university that helped prepare us for the thesis by conducting a systematic mapping study (SMS), which is a study which provides a broad overiew of the research area by analyzing existing studies. As mentioned, I have already decided to explore Agile in my thesis. At the same time, I was also (as most others were) interested in AI (because why not). However, AI and Agile is an extremely huge topic. To narrow the scope, I thought about what I was intrigued about and realised that communication within teams is important for me. At that point, I was curious about whether I, as a master student, could submit a research paper into a conference or journal for fun. I constantly consulted my supervisor and she encouraged me to try. One thing led to another and with the help of my co-author for double checking the research process and helping with the report, we submitted the paper to the AI and Agile workshop of XP2025. Thankfully, the paper was accepted and the authors were invited to speak during the conference. So, by becoming a speaker, I got to attend XP2025!

I presented on the first day of the conference, at the AI and Agile workshop. It was a full day workshop with attendance of around 20+ people. There were a mix of people, some who were extremely excited about the possibilities about AI and others who were afraid but knew its importance nevertheless.

Here are the links to the [final paper](https://zenodo.org/records/16146483) and [presentation](https://conf.researchr.org/details/xp-2025/ai-and-agile-2025/16/AI-and-Teamwork-in-Agile-Software-Development-A-Systematic-Mapping-Study).

The [published paper](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-032-05799-0_4) is also out!

## My reflections about the conference

Boy am I glad that I did a little retrospective session myself on the last day of XP2025 because it sure is tough to reflect on things 1 month ago. At least for me, its tough. What I really like about this conference is the inspiration and the ideas. I especially enjoyed the morning keynote speeches because they were delivered so thoughtfully and accurately. Many other talks I attended were also very thought provoking and motivating.

Below I will list some of the talks I attended from Day 2 to 4 (Day 1 was workshop day) and include points that I find interesting.

### Day 2 Keynote - AI for teams: State of play for AI assistance beyond code

By Birgitta Böckeler

- We don't really know what's going on in the blackbox of GenAI, so we need to learn from examples from others.
- GenAI is a great way to create prototype but don't use it to replace high fidelity prototypes.
- Context is key!
- Better do an integrity check.
- It seems like with AI, there is a lure back to waterfall but infact, because of AI, we need to be more resilient, responsive and have fast feedback loops!

### Day 3 Keynote - Five Years Into Remote and Hybrid Working – Has Anyone Wondered About Agile Teamwork?

By Darja Šmite

- Office presence paradox: The more we stay at home, the more disturbed we are in office.
- Every single interaction becomes a time boxed slot.
- We need to have more collaborative tasks to bring people back to office to work together!

### Day 4 Keynote - You Can't Ignore the Code: Coaching Development Teams

By Emily Bache

- We need technical excellence!
- Samman method helps improve code quality and agility of the organization.
- Progress through the skill acquisition model: familiarity, comprehension, conscious effort, conscious action, proficient, unconscious competence.
